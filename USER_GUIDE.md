# Music Wrapped - User Guide

## End User Usage

### Getting Started
1. **Login**
   - log in with existing credentials (you can check user_credentials.md as well)

2. **View Your Wrapped**
   - After login, you'll see the boot sequence animation
   - Scroll down to access the interactive terminal

3. **Using the Terminal**
   - Type `help` to see all available commands
   - Run queries to explore your music data:
     - `run total_plays` - View your total listening sessions
     - `run top_artists` - See your top 5 artists
     - `run top_songs` - Display your most played songs
     - `run top_albums` - Show your favorite albums
     - `run jam_sessions` - Find your music soulmate (who you listened with most)
     - `run mood_analysis` - Discover your dominant mood
     - `run top_genre` - See your sound identity
     - `run monthly_chart` - View your year in music activity
   - Type `clear` to reset the terminal

### Exploring the Music Library
- **Browse Artists**
  - Navigate to **History** 
  - Click on any artist to see their detailed profile
  
- **Browse Songs**
  - Navigate to **History** 
  - Click on any song to view details

## Content Manager Usage

### Access
- Login with content manager credentials
- Content Manager panel appears in navigation

### Managing Artists
1. Navigate to **Content Manager** → **Artists**
2. **Add New Artist:**
   - Click "Add New Artist"
   - Fill in: Name, Genre etc
   - Submit to create
3. **Edit Artist:**
   - Click "Edit" next to any artist
   - Update information and save
